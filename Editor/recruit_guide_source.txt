[Bastan]
where: Haud Village (appraisal shop)
how: After the restoration of Lordlake, carry a "? Painting" and talk to him in his appraisal shop in Haud Village, closing the appraise window each time without appraising the painting. The scene where he recognises it as a painting by Ivanov triggers at random (roughly one talk in ten), so keep talking to him until it fires, then agree to take him along.
missable: Missable, and his window closes earlier than most guides say. It shuts before the final battle for Sol-Falena: players holding saves from after Lord Barows' death could not trigger the scene at all (one account reports 100 failed attempts), while a save from just before the New Queen's Campaign recruited him on the 12th talk. Whether the exact trigger is Barows' death or the Stormfist siege is disputed, but both are well before Sol-Falena, so a player about to start the Sol-Falena battle is already too late. The official strategy guide's claim that recruitment stays open until the Sol-Falena siege is wrong.
